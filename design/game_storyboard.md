## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

Space Station Escape

**Storyline:**

Your spaceship has been sabotaged by a rogue artificial intelligence named NEXUS. You are trapped on a space station and must collect six critical components needed to regain control of the station and disable NEXUS. You will explore different rooms throughout the station, gathering items while avoiding the AI's control chamber. If you encounter NEXUS before collecting all six items, you lose. If you collect all six items and then confront NEXUS, you win.

## Rooms

Project One requires a minimum of eight rooms.

1. Command Center (Start Room)
2. Engineering Bay
3. Research Lab
4. Medical Bay
5. Communications Room
6. Cargo Hold
7. Crew Quarters
8. Observation Deck
9. Reactor Room

Add more rooms if your design needs them.

## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items.
Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. None
2. Plasma Wrench
3. Access Card
4. Med Kit
5. Signal Booster
6. Energy Cell
7. Security Codes
8. Villain: NEXUS
9. Navigation Chip

If you add rooms beyond the minimum, add an item for every additional room
except the start room and villain room.

## Villain

Villain: NEXUS

NEXUS is a rogue artificial intelligence that has taken control of the space station's systems. It has locked down critical areas, sabotaged the station, and trapped the player onboard. NEXUS resides in the Observation Deck and serves as the final threat in the game. The player must collect all six essential items needed to disable NEXUS before entering its room. If the player encounters NEXUS without all the items, they lose the game.
